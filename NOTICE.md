The clinical content is a paraphrased, structured summary of the American Diabetes Association
"Standards of Care in Diabetes-2026" (Diabetes Care 2026;49(Suppl. 1)), Sections 2, 3, 5, 6, 9, 10, 11 and 12.

The ADA licence permits educational, non-commercial use of the original text and prohibits reproduction,
text/data mining, and machine-learning use without written permission (permissions@diabetes.org).
Obtain permission before publishing this repository publicly or deploying it commercially.

Prices are US AWP/NADAC (15 July 2025) and are for relative comparison only.
This is decision-support data, not medical advice: a licensed clinician must verify every output.
